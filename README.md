# Electrify Fleets

Fleet electrification assessment for Flash Motors, served at
https://electrifyfleets.flash-motors.com through Cloudflare Pages.

- `index.html` is the landing page, interview and report.
- `assets/config.js` holds every price and assumption. Update numbers here only.
- `assets/calc.js` is the calculation.
- `assets/app.js` is the interview and report screens.

Pushing to `main` publishes the live site. Other branches get a private preview address.

Status: prototype. Figures marked PLACEHOLDER in `assets/config.js` are not real
Flash Motors prices and must be replaced before launch. Nothing is saved or emailed yet.
